Dr. Sneha Jain — Resident Ophthalmologist
About

Clinical precision, microsurgical dedication, and evidence-based eye care

Dr. Sneha Jain is an ophthalmologist based in Mumbai, currently completing her 3-year postgraduate residency at Jupiter Hospital, Thane. Her clinical foundation encompasses comprehensive eye care, diagnostic evaluation, and microsurgical training across high-volume tertiary outpatient clinics and surgical theaters.

Her primary surgical interest focuses on anterior segment microsurgery, specifically phacoemulsification, small-incision cataract surgery (SICS), pre-operative biometry, corneal evaluation, and anterior segment trauma management.

"Precision in anterior segment microsurgery relies on meticulous biometry, sound diagnostic judgment, and an absolute commitment to patient visual outcomes."

In addition to surgical training, Dr. Jain actively participates in clinical auditing, case presentations, and scientific research in anterior segment and general ophthalmology, aiming to combine clinical excellence with academic medicine as she prepares for subspecialty fellowship training.
