Top 5 Surgical Competencies

01 · Primary Anterior Segment Surgery
Phacoemulsification

Clear Corneal Phacoemulsification with Foldable IOL Implantation

Jupiter Hospital — Thane, Mumbai Region

Hands-on micro-incision cataract surgery performing clear corneal phacoemulsification using divide-and-conquer and stop-and-chop nuclear sculpting techniques. Extensive experience with continuous curvilinear capsulorhexis (CCC), automated cortical aspiration, and foldable hydrophobic acrylic intraocular lens (IOL) delivery in grade II-IV nuclear cataracts.

02 · High-Volume Cataract Surgery
Manual SICS

Small Incision Cataract Surgery (SICS) in Dense & Mature Cataracts

Jupiter Hospital — Thane & Community Surgical Camps

Primary surgical management of dense, brown, hyper-mature, and hard nuclear sclerosis cataracts via self-sealing sclero-corneal tunnel construction. Technique covers valvular tunnel creation, envelope capsulotomy / CCC, hydrodissection, nuclear prolapse into the anterior chamber, visco-expression, and rigid PMMA IOL placement with low induced astigmatism.

Primary Sclero-Corneal
PMMA Rigid IOL
03 · Refractive Cataract Planning
Biometry & Premium IOLs

Pre-Operative Optical Biometry & Toric / Premium IOL Planning

Jupiter Hospital — Thane

Specialized preoperative planning for cataract patients targeting spectacle independence. Utilizes swept-source OCT optical biometry (IOLMaster) alongside modern formulas (Barrett Universal II, Kane, Hill-RBF) for precise IOL power selection, astigmatism axis mapping for toric intraocular lenses, and refractive outcome auditing.

IOLMaster Optical Biometry
Barrett II Formula Calc

Pterygium Excision with Autologous Conjunctival Grafting

Jupiter Hospital & Grant Medical College OPDs

Surgical removal of primary and recurrent nasal/temporal pterygii with limbal-conjunctival autograft transplantation (CAG) secured via sutureless fibrin glue / 10-0 nylon micro-sutures to minimize recurrence rates and preserve corneal transparency.

Clinical Diversity

Case Spectrum & Problem Management

Exemplifying diagnostic and therapeutic decision-making across complex anterior segment conditions, ocular surface diseases, and emergency ophthalmic presentations.

Complex Cataracts

Hard Brown & Intumescent Cataracts

Pre-operative planning and intraoperative execution in grade V nuclear sclerosis, intumescent white cataracts requiring trypan blue staining for capsulorhexis, and small pupil phacoemulsification utilizing mechanical pupil expansion rings.

Refractive Cataract Surgery

Toric & Multifocal IOL Evaluation

Screening candidates for premium intraocular lenses using corneal topography, swept-source OCT biometry, posterior corneal astigmatism correction (Barrett Toric formula), and patient visual expectation counseling.

Corneal & Ocular Surface

Severe Dry Eye & Ocular Surface Disease

Diagnostic evaluation and stepped treatment for Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD), Sjögren's syndrome, persistent epithelial defects, punctal plug insertion, and autologous serum eye drops initiation.

Glaucoma Diagnostics

Primary & Secondary Glaucoma Management

Performing routine 4-mirror gonioscopy, Goldmann Applanation Tonometry (GAT), Humphrey Visual Field (HVF) visual field defect analysis, RNFL OCT interpretation, and medical pressure lowering therapy adjustment.

Emergency Ocular Trauma

Corneal Laceration & Hyphema Triage

Primary surgical closure of full-thickness corneal tears with 10-0 nylon monofilament, iris prolapse management, medical management of traumatic hyphema, intraocular pressure control, and secondary cataract timing.

Pediatric Eye Care

Pediatric Refraction & Amblyopia Workup

Cycloplegic retinoscopy in pediatric populations, amblyopia patching protocols, accommodative dynamics evaluation, and squints workup under senior pediatric ophthalmologist mentorship.
