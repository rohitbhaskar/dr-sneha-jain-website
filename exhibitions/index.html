Encrypted Subconscious

Noisy-le-Grand, Microcredit Paris, France

Research on interactions between viewers and neuroArt. Part of ongoing research on human interaction with artificial intelligence and the search for subconsciousness in machines. Participants documented observations below artworks — noticed symbols, architecture, situations, and archetypes. Data was fed to AI neural networks to train machine interpretation skills through human perception examples.

Enter the Void

Neo-Gothic Mansion — Bashmet Centre

A captivating showcase of colored glass sculptures demonstrating a masterful fusion of Gothic and futuristic aesthetics. The historical ambiance of the mansion perfectly complemented the modern designs of the glass artworks. Each piece, through its placement and the play of light, narrated a story of its own, casting vibrant colors and intriguing shadows that animated the grand old spaces. Live ambient and experimental music shows throughout the exhibition.

Club der Visionäre — Berlin

Club der Visionäre, Berlin, Germany

A vibrant and futuristic glass sculpture installation designed to reflect the dynamic energy and eclectic spirit of the venue. Each piece of colored glass is meticulously crafted to capture and play with natural and artificial lights. As sunlight streams through during the day, the installation casts a mosaic of vivid, dancing colors across the wooden beams. By night, the pieces glow under the club's lights, creating a flowing river of color that mirrors the famous waterfront setting.
