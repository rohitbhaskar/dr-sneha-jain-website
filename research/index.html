Primary Research Focus

Pediatric Ophthalmology & Strabismus Research

Mentored by Dr. Mihir Kothari — Consultant Paediatric Ophthalmologist & Squint Specialist, Jupiter Hospital

During her residency at Jupiter Hospital (Thane), Dr. Sneha Jain actively engaged in pediatric ophthalmology clinical research under the guidance of Dr. Mihir Kothari. Her work centered on pediatric refractive errors, amblyopia management protocols, accommodative dynamics, and non-invasive screening methods for binocular vision disorders in children.

  • Evaluated pharmacological and optical interventions for pediatric myopia control.
  • Assessed binocular vision anomalies, accommodative response, and strabismus screening accuracy in young patient populations.
  • Participated in prospective pediatric OPD observational cohorts and clinical data auditing.

Pediatric Myopia Progression & Accommodative Reserve

A longitudinal prospective study analyzing accommodative lag and axial length elongation in urban pediatric patients undergoing low-dose atropine therapy combined with optical correction.

Biometry Precision in Dense Nuclear Cataracts

Manuscript Under Review

Comparative clinical analysis evaluating swept-source OCT optical biometry versus immersion ultrasound for accurate intraocular lens (IOL) power selection in advanced cataracts.

Post-SICS Corneal Astigmatism & Wound Architecture
